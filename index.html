How the scoring works

Each finished session counts as one of these:

  • Good day: a green day where every exercise felt the same or better.
  • Held steady: an amber or red day, or one exercise felt slightly worse. Counts neither way.
  • Flare-up: any exercise felt much worse. You lose one good day, and tomorrow is capped at amber.

Collect 6 good days to move up to the next of the 5 stages, from calming the tendon to full activity. Steady days are fine: the rule is that you only add load once your elbow handles the current load well.
